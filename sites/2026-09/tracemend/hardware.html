The Pelton-Powered Bench Sequence

Our manufacturing lifecycle is intrinsically tied to the mountain topography. Every TraceMend circuit is built using 2.4 kW of continuous clean DC power, generated by the heavy glacial runoff spinning our antique 1894 Pelton wheel turbine.

Stage 01

Slate Block Slicing & Diamond Milling

We source raw off-cut blocks directly from the Blaenau quarries. The heavy slate is sliced using water-cooled diamond saws and precisely milled to accept our brass threaded inserts, forming a virtually indestructible outer shell.

Stage 02

Through-Hole Lead Bending & Soldering

Guided by brass calipers and paper schematics, every resistor, capacitor, and IC socket is manually bent and seated. The boards are hand-soldered using high-quality leaded rosin-core solder for maximum mechanical strength and thermal resilience.

Stage 03

Dual-Coat Conformal Beeswax Sealing

To defeat the relentless montane damp, each finished logic board is slowly lowered into a steaming pot of unrefined Welsh beeswax. This dual-coat process completely seals the copper traces from oxidative rot and condensation shorts.

Stage 04

Cryogenic Cold-Box Burn-In Test

Before a node leaves the winding house, it spends forty-eight hours inside our bespoke cold-box. Held at a sustained -25°C, we monitor the oscillator drift and battery sag to guarantee flawless performance when the real winter arrives.

Bespoke Quarry Enclosures & Slate Joinery

Modern plastics shatter when frozen and subjected to the violent impacts of gale-flung debris. Thin cast aluminium conducts extreme cold directly to the battery cells. We build with the geology of the mountain itself.

Our enclosures utilise 25 mm thick slabs of dense Blaenau slate. The sheer thermal inertia of this mass acts as a passive buffer, dramatically slowing the rate at which freezing external temperatures reach the internal electronics. This protects the battery chemistry from sudden thermal shock during rapid weather fronts.

Furthermore, the immense weight of the slate provides exceptional vibration damping against the intense mast shudder induced by 90-knot winds, preventing the destructive mechanical harmonics that rattle standard enclosures to pieces. Every slab is bound by marine-grade brass threaded inserts, guaranteeing that the case can be opened, repaired, and resealed endlessly without stripping the threads.

Hardware Selection & Maintenance FAQ

Why specify LiFePO4 battery chemistries over standard Lithium-Ion?

Lithium-Ion cells suffer severe voltage sag and permanent capacity loss when charged below freezing. LiFePO4 cells offer superior thermal stability, allowing safe charging curves even at -20°C, ensuring your node recovers power immediately when the morning sun breaks the clouds.

How do you protect the nodes from lightning strikes on exposed crags?

Every Ridge Node incorporates heavy-duty gas-discharge tube arrestors across the antenna lines. We provide thick braided copper grounding straps and instructions for driving earth spikes deep into the shallow mountain peat to safely shunt atmospheric electrical surges.

Can the antenna mast stays survive ice loading?

Yes. Our mast rigging doctrine relies on oversized stainless steel guy-wires and dynamic tensioners that accommodate the massive weight of rime ice buildup without snapping, allowing the ice to eventually fracture and shed during the thaw.

Is the firmware open for custom modification?

Absolutely. Every trace of code is licensed openly. You can re-flash the main microcontrollers in the field via an exposed internal serial header using nothing but a standard USB TTL cable and an open-source terminal application.

Are replacement components available if I damage the board?

We publish a complete Bill of Materials (BOM) alongside our KiCad schematics. Every resistor, capacitor, and regulator is a standard through-hole part available from major global electronics suppliers. You are never locked into our supply chain.
