Material Specification Matrix

No consumer shortcuts are taken during our manufacturing cycle. The Hardpan Outfitter sources materials strictly to industrial rail and maritime standards to prevent failure in the field.

1680D TPU-Laminated Nylon

Our baseline textile offers extreme abrasion resistance. The thermoplastic polyurethane lamination provides a total barrier against hydrocarbons and brine, refusing to crack at temperatures down to -45°C.

Laser-Cut Grade 316 Stainless Steel

All load-bearing hardware, including D-rings and tension buckles, is fabricated from marine-grade 316 stainless steel to ensure zero corrosion during prolonged salt exposure.

Cryogenic Thread Stitching

Seams are bonded using specialised cryogenic thread that maintains flexibility and tensile strength under extreme temperature fluctuations, preventing seam bursts under heavy dynamic loads.

Santoprene Edge Bumpers

High-impact zones are shielded with oil-resistant Santoprene thermoplastic vulcanisate. These bumpers absorb kinetic shocks when equipment is dropped onto concrete quaysides or granite ballast.

Operational Field Protocols

Proper operational deployment prevents equipment loss and secures transit loads. These protocols must be followed strictly when operating in severe sub-zero environments.

Pre-Rig Anchor Check

Before applying tension, inspect all drop-forged shackles and 316 stainless D-rings for grit accumulation. Ensure the anchor points are free from ice build-up. Apply load gradually to confirm engagement before full torque is applied.

Thermal Gasket Seating Protocol

In sub-zero conditions, elastomeric seals require firm, even pressure to seat correctly. Depress the lid evenly across all latches simultaneously. Do not force single latches down independently, as this may pinch the cold gasket and compromise the IP68 rating.

Ballast-Drag Tie-Down Pattern

When securing the Mark IV Haul Sled for transit over scree or ballast, use the reinforced cross-strap configuration. Tension the load evenly across the 12-tonne bridle to distribute abrasion forces across the Santoprene edge bumpers.

Post-Exposure Washdown Guidelines

Following deployment in coastal or maritime environments, all gear must undergo fresh water washdown to dissolve salt crystals. Pay special attention to the stainless steel closure lugs to prevent abrasive wear on the moving parts.

Technical & Dispatch FAQ

Clear, direct answers to technical hurdles regarding cold-soak tolerances and trade dispatch.

How does the material handle sub-zero thermal brittleness?

The TPU-laminated 1680D nylon is chemically formulated to resist thermal brittleness. It retains its flexibility and will not crack even when subjected to prolonged cold-soak conditions down to -45°C.

What is required for saltwater corrosion maintenance?

Our hardware utilises grade 316 stainless steel, significantly reducing maintenance overhead. We simply recommend a standard fresh water washdown after extended exposure to dissolve accumulated salt deposits.

Do you supply field puncture repair kits?

Yes, rapid deployment repair kits are available for all vulcanised gear. They contain specialized cryogenic adhesives and ballistic patches to execute temporary structural fixes directly in the field.

Are these cases rated for hazardous material containment?

Our IP68 rated Sub-Deck Dry Pods meet strict municipal containment guidelines. They provide a secure barrier against fluid ingress or egress when dealing with sensitive industrial materials.
