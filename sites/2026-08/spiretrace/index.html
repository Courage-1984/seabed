01 // THE VERTICAL FIELD LABORATORY

Rupicolous lichens, calcicolous bryophytes, and cliff-dwelling vascular plants occupy some of the least surveyed microclimates in Western Europe. Standard mountaineering techniques are designed for rapid upward movement and destructive protection placement, frequently damaging the delicate vegetative crust that researchers travel to inspect. SpireTrace re-engineers big-wall climbing logistics into stable, low-impact scientific observation platforms that can remain anchored in place across five-day weather windows.

Our field teams deploy custom-fabricated double portaledge stations equipped with wind-deflecting ripstop rainflies, lithium-iron-phosphate field power, and vibration-damped optical mounting arms.

By balancing anchor loads across four redundant non-penetrating cam arrays and broad-webbing tree slings, we achieve sixty-kilonewton system strength while leaving zero metal hardware or chalk residue on the rock face after de-rigging.

Scientists work in full body harnesses with twin-rope backup lines, accessing sheer dolerite columns and limestone overhangs with micro-positioning ascenders. We manage all vertical haulage, meteorology monitoring, and emergency evacuation protocols, allowing botanists to focus entirely on high-magnification field microscopy and genetic tissue sampling.

02 // VERTICAL SURVEY DISCIPLINES

Precision access configurations developed exclusively for prolonged botanical taxonomy and high-angle microclimate research missions.

SUSPENDED PORTALEDGE HABITATS

Enclosed two-person cliff platforms engineered for prolonged stationary taxonomy. These modular systems feature insulated thermal floorboards preventing convection heat loss, internal microclimate data loggers, and exterior solar recharging arrays capable of sustaining critical lab equipment. Designed for consecutive seventy-two hour vertical deployments, permitting uninterrupted taxonomic categorisation directly on the cliff face.

ZERO-IMPACT ACCESS RIGGING

Advanced non-penetrating clamping arrays and equalised static lines designed explicitly to prevent spalling on fragile limestone and sandstone cliffs. We employ broad surface-area dyneema webbing and tension-distributed mechanical cams to ensure delicate petrophytic flora remain entirely undisturbed. Our rigging guarantees zero permanent trace, satisfying the strictest requirements of national heritage trusts.

HIGH-RESOLUTION MACRO PHOTOGRAMMETRY

We deploy calibrated 100mm optical camera rigs mounted on micro-geared positioning heads, enabling precise 3D digital surface reconstruction of lichen crusts in situ. Integrated illumination arrays provide forensic-grade lighting against dark basalt formations, allowing taxonomists to capture apothecia structures and spore dispersion patterns with unparalleled metric accuracy.
