01 // THE CORNISH-MIDLANDS DEFENCE PACT

In 2019, Newlyn Net Syndicate and Stourbridge Armourworks merged shop floors to resolve a persistent material failure: standard offshore waxed canvas tears under steel cable shear, while tactical aramid vests rot rapidly under saltwater exposure. The joint syndicate operates out of Hangar 4 at Falmouth Docks, weaving continuous-filament ultra-high-molecular-weight polyethylene trawl twine directly into Kevlar 129 structural frames.

The resulting textile resists rot, diesel immersion, and high-velocity abrasive contact. Seams are joined using double-pass lock-stitches of braided Dyneema and vulcanised with maritime neoprene tape, eliminating raw needle perforations. Hardware fittings are forged from 316-grade marine stainless steel, drop-tested under 20-kilonewton static shock loads.

Our production capacity is fixed at 120 units per calendar month. Each piece receives an individual cold-stamped serial plate and destructive batch sample testing records before release to industrial logistics chains.

REQUISITION PROTOCOLS & TECHNICAL FAQ

What is the minimum order quantity for commercial trade accounts?
Our production capacity limits output to 120 units per calendar month. Individual operators may reserve a single unit, while trade accounts require a minimum allocation request of five garments.
How does sizing accommodate heavy maritime layering?
All garments are structurally oversized by precisely 30 millimetres at the chest and shoulder blocks. Do not size up. Order your measured dimension to permit heavy thermal undergarments beneath the shell.
What are the maintenance and re-proofing protocols?
Machine wash cycles will destroy the vulcanised tape structure. Hose down the exterior with fresh water to clear surface salt. For heavy diesel saturation, utilize our authorized industrial solvent protocol before returning to the field.
Do you provide international maritime shipping?
Dispatch originates directly from Falmouth Docks. We coordinate logistics with commercial freight carriers across the North Sea and Atlantic. Clients are responsible for all port customs clearance documentation upon arrival.
How does the 10-year repair service function?
Any garment suffering structural puncture or seam delamination within a decade of its cold-stamped serial date must be shipped back to Hangar 4. Our technicians will re-stitch the damaged zone with braided Dyneema and re-vulcanise the seal.
