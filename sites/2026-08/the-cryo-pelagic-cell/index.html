Technical & Medical Screening

Is prior cold-shock habituation required?

Yes. We expect personnel to possess baseline cold tolerance before entering the flume. Operators lacking recent exposure will undergo a strict step-down acclimation protocol before being cleared for the -2.4°C core soak.

What are the absolute medical contraindications?

Individuals with diagnosed Raynaud’s syndrome, uncontrolled hypertension, or a history of severe cardiac arrhythmias are permanently barred from the sub-zero flume. Full medical history is vetted by our clinical officers prior to arrival.

Can I clear my ears for the hyperbaric stage?

The 1.4 ATA chamber mandates active Valsalva or Toynbee manoeuvres. If you suffer from chronic sinus blockages or compromised Eustachian tubes, you will not pass the initial pressure test and will be removed from Phase 02.

What thermal gear is provided on site?

We supply heavy neoprene extremities protection (booties and gloves) to prevent frostbite during the liquid immersion phase. All other recovery garments, including post-soak thermal layers, are issued directly from the bunker armoury.

Is direct transport from Sumburgh Airport available?

Yes. We deploy rugged transport shuttles directly from the Sumburgh airstrip to the Scalloway bunker. Flight manifests and ETA must be submitted with your initial requisition packet.
