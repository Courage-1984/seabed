Cornish Metallurgy & Workshop Materials

Crafted from Proven Admiralty Materials

We source all metal stock and timber dunnage through regional UK certified merchants. No plastic fillers, no toxic lead, and no unstable resin composites.

58/42 Bismuth-Tin Eutectic

Lead-free casting alloy with a density of 8.56 g/cm³ and a liquidus point of 138°C. Melts cleanly in small gas ladles and cools without shrinkage cavities, ensuring strict mass tolerance within ±0.1g per block.

CZ121 Turned Marine Brass

High-tensile free-machining naval brass rod turned on Colchester bench lathes at Custom-House Quay. Retains sharp knurled cross-heads and resists saltwater corrosion in coastal chartrooms.

Cornish Boxwood & Cedar

Kiln-dried native boxwood provides rigid, non-splintering dunnage wedges for container tiers. Packed in aromatic cedar shavings that prevent moisture condensation inside the steel tins.

Enamelled Steel Tinplate

Heavyweight 0.28mm electrolytic tinplate cases coated in high-bake oxblood enamel. Rolled safety edges, heavy-gauge piano hinges, and secure spring snap clasps rated for 10,000 cycles.
