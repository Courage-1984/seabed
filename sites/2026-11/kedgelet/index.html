The Bismuth-Tin Ballast Bench • Metallurgical Precision

Heavy metal shrunk to thumb-size, balanced to the tenth of a gram.

Standard wooden model blocks float like corks and tell a pilot nothing about cargo-stack top-heaviness. At Kedgelet, we alloy bismuth and tin in small ladle batches so each 1:250 container block scales down the true centre-of-gravity of a loaded 40-foot box. You can stack four tiers high on a pocket barge hull and watch the exact heel angle settle on a brass inclinometer.

Every casting is tumbled in crushed walnut shell, primed in matte buoy-white, and hand-dipped into high-visibility tomato red or electric cobalt to mark dangerous-goods and reefer bays. We stamp the gross tonnage ratio straight into the baseplate while the alloy is still warm from the mould.

The result fits inside a coat pocket yet behaves like steel on the water—or across an oiled chartroom slate when the wind picks up outside the breakwater.

Standard Admiralty Table Procedure

The Four-Step Quay Berthing Rehearsal

How senior Trinity House pilots and coastal tug masters run table drills before taking a coaster off the Carrick Roads anchorage into Falmouth Inner Harbour.

01

Position the Tide Slate

Lay the wet-ground Delabole slate squarely across your admiralty chart. Orient the magnetic quay rule along your target berthing heading, accounting for cross-channel ebb eddies setting off Pendennis Point.

02

Pin the Turned Bollards

Place your 14mm brass bitts at your certified quay spacing. Neodymium base magnets clamp firmly through the slate backplate, preventing bollard slip when miniature warps come under load.

03

Stack Weighted Bays

Configure your vessel's cargo manifest using 42g bismuth-tin blocks. Place dangerous-goods red bays low and reefer cobalt bays outboard to observe natural vessel heel and metacentric stability on the cradle.

04

Pull the Spring Line

Run braided hemp spring lines between the bow bitt and quay cleat. Simulate engine kick and rudder wash to test whether your vessel pivots smoothly without fender compression crush.
