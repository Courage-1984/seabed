Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, honest answers about road regulations, waste disposal licensing, and how we fix drains without shutting roads.

How do you comply with Chapter 8 street-works legislation without deploying cones?
Chapter 8 of the Traffic Signs Manual mandates that highway work must provide clear pedestrian walkways and safe physical separation from live traffic lanes. Because our cargo trikes measure just 1.2 metres wide, we position our rigs entirely within designated kerbside cycleways or footway margins using high-intensity Class 1 reflective chevrons and fold-out tactile warning ramps. We create a self-contained pedestrian buffer zone that protects passing pram and wheelchair users without stealing carriageway space from traffic.
Where does extracted street silt and chemical runoff go?
All extracted road silt, heavy gravel, and decomposed urban slurry are sealed in our onboard 240-litre double-baffled cassettes under continuous negative pressure. At the end of every morning and afternoon run, trikes return to Arch 12 on Sloper Road where waste is dewatered, filtered, and certified for disposal under our Natural Resources Wales Waste Carrier Licence #CBDU-44109-CYM. Extracted solids are recycled into inert aggregate, and treated water is returned to our closed-loop washing system.
Can a 180-bar rotary nozzle cut through dense tree-root blockages?
Yes. Cardiff's mature plane and lime trees frequently send fibrous root hairpins straight through vitrified clay pipe collars into gully traps. Our 180-bar rotary brass nozzle spins at over 3,000 RPM, acting as a micro-milling tool that slices dense organic mats into centimetre-scale particles without cracking the underlying drainage pipework. For severe structural root intrusions beyond thirty millimetres diameter, we document the defect with high-resolution borescope cameras for council pipe-replacement crews.
How does your polymer bedding mortar cure under heavy Welsh rain?
Conventional sand-and-cement mortar washes out if it rains within six hours of installation, leaving iron grates unsupported and prone to sinking. We use moisture-activated thixotropic polyurethane and epoxy-polymer resins engineered specifically for marine environments. Ambient moisture actually catalyses the hardening reaction, reaching a compressive strength of 50 N/mm² in twenty minutes even when submerged in puddle water.
Do you service private estate car parks and commercial loading bays?
Absolutely. In addition to adopted public cycle lanes and council kerbs, we maintain private drainage networks for business improvement districts, supermarket service yards, harbourside apartment estates, and independent retail parks across Cardiff and the Vale of Glamorgan. Private estate managers frequently retain us because our trikes can enter underground car parks and height-restricted loading bays where twelve-tonne suction lorries physically cannot fit.
What is your guaranteed emergency response time during storm warnings?
For holders of our High-Street Trader Pass and BID Audit Subscriptions, we maintain a guaranteed sixty-minute on-site arrival window during Met Office yellow and amber rain warnings across Cardiff. For standard one-off bookings logged through our portal, our average dispatch-to-kerb arrival time is forty-one minutes within our primary operating radius.
