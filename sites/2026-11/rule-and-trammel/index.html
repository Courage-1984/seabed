    MECHANICAL GEOMETRY & PROVENANCE

    One-twentieth of a millimetre, cut by leadscrew and burin.

    SPECIFICATION ARCHIVE §1924

    A dividing engine works without optics or software: a hardened steel leadscrew advances the carriage by a fixed fraction of a turn while a weighted diamond-tipped burin scores the face of the rule. Because the cut is mechanical, every graduation line holds a crisp V-shaped trench that catches graphite and dividers' points alike.

    Before any blank reaches the carriage, European boxwood billets rest for seven years on slatted oak racks in our Sheffield loft until moisture stabilises below nine percent. Only straight-grained, knot-free lengths pass to the planer and brass-binding press.

    We still cut twelfths, sixteenths, and Admiralty spar-taper ratios alongside standard millimetres, giving boatwrights and restoration joiners exact replacements for nineteenth-century lofting tables.

    TECHNICAL CARE & WORKSHOP POLICIES

    Care, graduations, and parcel dispatch

    Practical advice on maintaining boxwood blades, ordering bespoke spar tapers, and our Sheffield recalibration service.

    How should European boxwood rules be oiled and stored at the workbench? +

    Rub the boxwood blade lightly with raw cold-pressed linseed oil once every six months, wiping off all excess with a clean linen rag after twenty minutes. Never soak the wood or expose it to direct heat lamps. Boxwood's naturally dense, fine cellular structure repels workshop grime when properly seasoned.

    Can an instrument be returned to Sheffield for re-scribing or recalibration? +

    Yes. We offer lifetime maintenance for all Rule & Trammel blades. If heavy workshop wear softens a graduation line after years of knife scoring, return the rule to our Kelham Island shop. We clean the blade, lightly dress the edge, and re-trace the V-grooves on our 1924 engine for a £24 bench fee.

    How are instruments packed for UK and international postal dispatch? +

    Every rule and trammel set is wrapped in anti-corrosive VCI tissue, enclosed in a heavy waxed cardboard tube or oiled canvas roll, and shipped in double-walled parcel crates. UK orders travel via Royal Mail Tracked 24; international deliveries ship via DHL Express with signed calibration certificates included.

    Why are 1/12th duodecimal graduations preferred over metric by traditional timber craftspeople? +

    Traditional British timber frame construction and heritage joinery rely on duodecimal divisibility: twelve divides evenly by two, three, four, and six without infinite decimal fractions. Scribed twelfths allow joiners to step off proportions directly with dividers rather than calculating awkward fractions.

    Will the sand-cast CZ121 brass fittings tarnish over time? +

    CZ121 brass naturally develops a warm, dull bronze patina from hand handling and workshop atmospheric exposure. This oxidised surface layer protects the underlying alloy without altering mechanical tolerances. If you prefer bright metal, buff lightly with micro-abrasive polishing paste.
