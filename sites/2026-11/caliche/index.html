Frequently Asked Questions

What are the terms for the £45 brass tin deposit refund? +

Your forty-five-pound deposit is refunded in full to your original payment card the moment your 24-Pan Caliche Field Tin arrives back at our Hay-on-Wye workshop. We don't penalise scratches, paint stains, or trail patina—we only require that the brass hinge and thumb-ring remain structurally sound.

How do international pan swaps work for painters outside the UK? +

European Union painters can deposit spent pans at our Tabernas locker or use our pre-addressed Madrid postal depot. For overseas shipments beyond Europe, we supply pre-stamped international return sachets with standard five-day tracked dispatch.

Is there a vegan binder formulation available instead of honey? +

Yes. Upon request during checkout or tier renewal, we mull your full 24-pan set using purely plant-derived food-grade vegetable glycerine and Kordofan gum arabic rather than wildflower honey. The re-wetting speed and colour saturation remain completely identical in the field.

Can I select custom regional pigment sets or swap specific single pans? +

Yes. While new tins ship with our balanced standard 24-pan terrain palette, your swap requests can specify any mix of available quarry batches. If your work focuses strictly on red sandstone or limestone flora, you can swap six burnt ochres or raw siennas at a time.

What happens if a tin is lost, damaged, or dropped down a scree slope? +

If your tin is lost or unrecoverable on the mountain, your initial forty-five-pound deposit covers the hardware replacement cost without further penalty. You can then place a replacement deposit to check out a new tin and continue your active annual swap pass.
