Zero-crinkle cotton that outwaits a six-hour Norfolk squall.

Ultralight nylon tarps shed rain well enough until a gust catches the hem and turns your shelter into a percussion instrument. In a hide overlooking a heronry or a reedbed, that metallic slap sends every bird within two hundred yards into the next parish.

PegLow patterns every shelter from 180-gram Scottish dry-waxed cotton ripstop woven in Dundee. The beeswax-and-paraffin finish holds back 8,000 millimetres of water without stiff plastic laminates, so the cloth drapes softly over a single carbon pole and stays dead silent when the wind shifts.

We replace every zipper and velcro tab with hand-turned horn toggles and brass-rimmed tripod ports, letting you swing a spotting scope or open a sketchbook in driving drizzle without a single click.

Unlike modern polyurethane coatings that perish into sticky yellow flakes after three damp winters in an unheated potting shed, genuine Scottish dry-wax ripstop patinas gracefully. If a briar thorn snags an edge on the walk out to Blakeney Point, the tear stops dead at the cross-grain reinforcement grid. A warm rub with our paraffin bee-block restores full hydrostatic resilience before the afternoon tide turns.

INLINE PROCESS DEMO // DEPLOYMENT PROTOCOL

Pitched silently in ninety seconds, without a single mallet blow

Cold fingers and driving North Sea sleet are poor companions for complex guy-line geometries or stubborn tent pegs. The PegLow single-pole hide is patterned to tension itself through geometry alone, anchoring securely into soft coastal mud using your own body weight.

Peg the Windward Corners

Unroll the shelter directly downwind. Step firmly onto the two broad matte-anodised 7075 aluminium corner stakes. The barbed stake heads sink three inches into damp peat without a hammer strike or metallic chime.

02

Raise the Carbon Mast

Slip the four-section Easton carbon mast through the central internal sleeve. Guide the rounded brass ferrule into the reinforced mast cap with one smooth upward sweep. The pyramid snaps into self-tensioning tautness.

03

Toggle the Tripod Skirt

Pass your spotting scope or camera lens through the silent forward aperture. Loop three hand-turned buffalo horn toggles through the oiled braided cotton cords. Zero velcro tearing noise; total windproof sealing.
