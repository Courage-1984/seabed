FLAGSHIP SYSTEM

The Polaris Parka — A System, Not a Garment

The Polaris Parka is the result of three winters of direct feedback from glaciologists on the Austfonna ice cap. Its outer shell is a 3-layer nylon ripstop with a hydrostatic head of 30,000 mm, seam-taped and tested in driving sleet at –35°C. Insulation uses a box-baffle construction filled with 800-fill-power European goose down, treated with a permanent fluorocarbon-free hydrophobic finish. The hood accommodates a Petzl Vertex helmet without restricting peripheral vision, and the expedition-length two-way front zip is rated to 10,000 cycles at –40°C. Each parka ships with a numbered service log, spare zip sliders and a repair patch kit inside the left chest pocket. Weight: 1,480 g in size L. Colours: safety orange and un-dyed grey. No logo on the exterior — only a small embroidered latitude marker inside the collar.

POLAR LOGISTICS

Packed, Sealed, Flown Out

Operating directly from Depot 4 in Longyearbyen places our operational supply chain at 78°13′ North, inside the reality of the Arctic climate. Every order is physically inspected and packed by field technicians inside our converted shipping-container depot. To neutralize ambient condensation during polar air freight, gear is vacuum-sealed inside rugged moisture-proof barriers before transfer into transit cases.

Before final enclosure, each shipment receives a signal flare and a latitude-stamped dispatch log recording barometric pressure, surface temperature and technician initials. Consignments are formally handed over to air cargo carriers within twenty-four hours. Flights connect through mainland Norway via Tromsø, maintaining dependable schedules to remote staging stations. Lead times average three business days to Tromsø and six working days for international research staging facilities.

SURVIVAL STANDARDS

Tested Where It Counts

We do not rely on laboratory test chambers or theoretical thermal modeling. True gear survival can only be validated through direct exposure to high-velocity polar gales and abrasive snow scour. Our equipment inventory undergoes continuous seasonal field trials conducted with glaciology research stations and veteran Svalbard logistics guides.

Our primary verification benchmark is the Nordaustlandet Winter Traverse: a continuous seventy-two-hour unsupported solo crossing across exposed glacial moraines at sustained ambient temperatures of –38°C with wind gusts exceeding twenty-four metres per second. To achieve operational compliance, gear must satisfy uncompromising pass criteria: zero structural seam deformation under load, zero mechanical zipper hesitation after repeated salt-rime ice immersion, and zero internal insulation drift across prolonged blizzard exposure.

FIELD QUERIES

Common Questions from the Field

How do I arrange deliveries to remote Arctic field camps?

We coordinate logistics with coastal support vessels and chartered flights departing Longyearbyen or Tromsø. Specify your radio callsign and staging coordinates during checkout. Our team packages equipment in sealed waterproof transport containers to ensure reliable airstrip handovers without weather exposure.

What occurs if a piece of equipment suffers field damage?

Because our systems are engineered for severe environmental stress, every Polaris Parka ships with a specialized repair patch kit and spare zipper sliders. For structural damage after extended deployment, we provide direct depot exchange services via mainland supply flights at zero maintenance fee.

How do portable power banks maintain capacity in extreme wind chill?

Standard lithium cells suffer voltage drop below –20°C. Our specialized power units employ vacuum-sealed graphene insulation barriers with internal thermal cycling. This construction maintains cell equilibrium autonomously, preserving over ninety percent battery capacity during extended surface operations down to –45°C.

What currency, billing methods, and institutional research invoicing options are accepted?

We accept electronic wire transfer, institutional credit authorization, or official research foundation purchase orders processed through Tromsø banks. We routinely supply university departments and polar institutes, providing tax-exempt export documents and latitude-stamped shipping manifests to satisfy strict institutional accounting regulations.

What are your return and depot replacement timelines across polar transport routes?

If an item fails to match your technical specifications prior to deployment, returns are processed via our partner terminal in Tromsø. Replacement units are dispatched by cargo air within twenty-four hours of arrival, and refunds execute upon verification of intact moisture seals.
