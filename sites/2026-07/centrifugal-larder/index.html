Section 02 // Core Principles

The Physics of Non-Thermal Clarification

The standard culinary consommé requires prolonged simmering and egg-white rafting, a process that inherently destroys volatile aromatic compounds through prolonged heat exposure. We circumvent thermal breakdown entirely via mechanical sheer force.

By processing raw ingredients through a decanter centrifuge at 15,000 RPM, we separate water, lipid, and solid phases at the molecular level within minutes. The resulting liquid phase is then passed through a 0.2-micron cross-flow filtration membrane, yielding an optically flawless liquid that retains one hundred percent of its raw aromatic profile. This non-thermal clarification is particularly critical for fragile botanicals, raw alliums, and unroasted mushroom profiles, where traditional heat applications introduce unwanted caramelization or oxidation. Our signature Absolute Cèpe isolate requires zero reduction to achieve maximum umami density. The output is a highly concentrated, sterile, and standardized culinary medium that eliminates forty hours of kitchen labor per week, allowing chefs to measure complex organic flavors in precise millilitres rather than unpredictable batches.

Section 05 // Cavitation Engineering

Cavitation-Stabilised Sauces

Creating permanently bonded culinary sauces without deploying synthetic chemical thickeners requires overcoming interfacial surface tension through intense mechanical acoustics. Our inline ultrasonic titanium sonotrode systems inject continuous high-frequency acoustic energy at twenty kilocycle wavelengths per second into immiscible oil and water mixtures.

Acoustic cavitation fractures lipid spheres below 200 nanometres, constructing an electrostatically permanent emulsion matrix that defies gravity and temperature separation.

The practical operational result is a mechanically unbreakable culinary base engineered specifically for rigorous commercial kitchen service. Classic hollandaise, mayonnaise, beurre blanc, and citrus vinaigrette fluid structures generated through sound wave cavitation resist phase separation even when exposed to direct overhead quartz service heat lamps for hours or subjected to repetitive rapid freeze-thaw blast chilling cycles during advanced prep staging.

Section 07 // Technical Parameters

Storage & Technical Parameters

Empirical specification data addressing common procurement inquiries from executive chefs, purchasing directors, and laboratory research managers.

Q // What is the validated shelf life of 0.2-micron filtered liquid culinary isolates?

Through absolute microbiological exclusion via 0.2-micron ceramic membranes within our positive-pressure cleanroom, enzymatic and vegetative bacterial spoilage vectors are entirely removed. Unopened amber glass containers remain chemically and aromatically stable for up to six months un-refrigerated when stored away from direct ultraviolet irradiance in ambient storehouse temperatures between ten and twenty-two degrees Celsius. Once unsealed, chilled storage at four degrees Celsius preserves volatility for sixty operational days.

Q // What are the minimum order quantities (MOQ) and lead times for bespoke ingredient extractions?

For proprietary kitchen recipes requiring custom decanter fractionation or ultrasonic homogenization, our laboratory intake requires a minimum raw feedstock input volume of ten kilograms per operational production batch. Standard processing turnaround from frozen or chilled intake delivery at our Milton Keynes facility to analytical dispatch requires four working days, supplied exclusively in 500ml pharmaceutical vessels.

Q // What are the specific chemical and pH parameters of the clarified tomato water isolate?

Our clarified tomato aqueous fraction exhibits an optical refraction profile of exactly zero turbidity with a measured titratable acidity averaging four point two pH at twenty degrees Celsius. Total dissolved solids remain stabilized between six and eight degrees Brix, containing concentrated natural l-glutamic acid while completely rejecting skin pigments and cellulosic pectin pulp.

Q // How do your shipping protocols enforce temperature stability and cold-chain compliance?

While sterile filtered aqueous extracts survive un-refrigerated transit without spoilage, vulnerable herbal essences and volatile lipid extracts are dispatched in high-density vacuum-insulated thermal containers charged with dry ice or regenerative eutectic refrigerant plates. Real-time digital temperature loggers accompany all shipments to prove zero thermal spikes occurred during transit from Buckinghamshire to your culinary receiving bay.

Q // What rigorous physical allergen cross-contamination protocols regulate cleanroom apparatus?

Following every production run, all stainless steel contact surfaces, decanter centrifuge rotors, and titanium cavitation sonotrodes undergo clean-in-place alkaline and organic acid sanitisation protocols. Rinse fluids are routinely tested via high-performance liquid chromatography and rapid ATP swabs to confirm zero residue from crustaceans, nuts, alliums, or soy before initiating subsequent culinary ingredient extractions.
